AI systems in sensitive areas such as health care, credit, employment and criminal justice are often held back from deployment until their internals can be explained. This paper argues that this asks more of mechanistic interpretability than it can deliver. It proposes instead a *calibrated verification* gate: authorization limited to a specific domain and use, independently checkable, monitored after release, and backed by accountability, contestability and revocation. It also introduces *Verification Coverage*, a six-component metric meant to be reported alongside capability scores in model cards, leaderboards and regulatory disclosures.
